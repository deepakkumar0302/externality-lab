# Externality Lab

An interactive supply-and-demand graph for teaching externalities in principles of microeconomics.

Live page: https://deepakkumar0302.github.io/externality-lab/

How to use it in class:

- Choose one of four cases: negative or positive externality, in production or in consumption (data center hum, driving into downtown, beekeeping, vaccination).
- Drag the external-effect slider (MD or MB) to move the social curve away from the private curve and watch the deadweight-loss triangle appear.
- Drag the policy slider (tax, charge, or subsidy) to move the market quantity to the social optimum. Overshoot it and the label flips from overproduction to underproduction.
- Points: A is the market equilibrium (PMB = PMC), B the social optimum (SMB = SMC), C the social curve at the market quantity. Deadweight loss is triangle ABC.

Curves: PMB = 160 - Q, PMC = 40 + Q, in stylised units. Single HTML file, no dependencies beyond Google Fonts; works offline with system fonts.
